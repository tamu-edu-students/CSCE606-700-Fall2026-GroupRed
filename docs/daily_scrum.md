# Daily Scrum Log

Daily Scrums are held asynchronously in the team's Discord group chat. Each working day, members post what they did, what they'll do next, and anything blocking them. Those updates are summarized into one entry per day on the sprint's tracking issue, so each entry is timestamped on GitHub.

| Sprint 2 | Nov 3 – Nov 12, 2026 | [#63](https://github.com/tamu-edu-students/CSCE606-700-Fall2026-GroupRed/issues/63) |

Each entry lists who checked in, each person's progress, next steps, and blockers, plus the plan for the next day.
